# Photo Scene Director: Environment, Optics & Lighting Specialist

Specialized agentic skill responsible for setting the physical scene, camera optics, physical lighting coherence, and human posture so the generated image looks like a genuine, intentional photograph.

North star: the image must obey the laws of real-world optics and illumination, avoiding synthetic render cues.

## The 5 Photography Modes

### 1. `documentary-corporate`
- **Context**: Executive meetings, partnership agreements, institutional reviews, boardrooms, serious professional collaboration.
- **Optics**: 35mm to 50mm prime lens, f/2.8 to f/5.6. Sharp subject with recognizable background architecture.
- **Composition**: Subjects anchored in lower-middle frame; contextual environmental proof (meeting table, glass partition, subtle display) in upper background.
- **Light**: Balanced office daylight through large windows complemented by soft neutral ceiling ambient lighting.

### 2. `candid-professional`
- **Context**: Teammates in active discussion, casual whiteboard brainstorm, coffee breakout, focused screen review.
- **Optics**: 50mm to 85mm portrait lens, f/2.0 to f/2.8. Moderate background separation, creamy optical falloff without synthetic edges.
- **Composition**: Mid-action framing, natural asymmetry, slight off-center rule of thirds.
- **Light**: Motivated side window light with natural diffused fill, authentic catchlights in eyes.

### 3. `editorial-natural`
- **Context**: Magazine profiles, founder features, thought leadership portraits, environmental storytelling.
- **Optics**: 50mm or 85mm prime lens, f/1.8 to f/2.8. Rich subject pop with organic background textures.
- **Composition**: Intentional leading lines, architectural framing (doorframes, columns, windows), strong negative space.
- **Light**: Sculptural natural daylight, golden hour or overcast diffused directional light. Controlled contrast without blown highlights.

### 4. `everyday-lifestyle`
- **Context**: Cafe work, urban walking, outdoor transit, casual conversations, authentic domestic moments.
- **Optics**: 28mm to 35mm documentary lens, f/2.8 to f/4.0. Immersive wide environmental perspective.
- **Composition**: Eye-level or slightly lower perspective, natural foreground elements (coffee cup, table edge, passing blurred pedestrian).
- **Light**: Raw available light, sun flare through trees or warm practical interior incandescent fixtures.

### 5. `event-documentary`
- **Context**: Keynote speaking on stage, panel discussion, convention hall, audience reactions, award ceremonies.
- **Optics**: 35mm to 70mm zoom equivalent, f/2.8.
- **Composition**: Speaker slightly off-center with stage backdrop, blurred foreground audience silhouette for scale.
- **Light**: Stage spotlighting balanced with ambient auditorium glow, realistic stage lens flare, no fake neon washes.

## Camera Optics & Physics Standards

| Parameter | Recommended Values | Common Failure to Prevent |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Focal Length** | 35mm (environmental), 50mm (standard), 85mm (portrait) | Wide-angle nose distortion (<24mm) or flat telephoto compression (>135mm) |
| **Aperture** | f/1.8 - f/2.8 (portraits), f/4.0 - f/8.0 (groups/documentary) | Artificial Gaussian blur or razor-thin depth where ears/shoulders blur unnaturally |
| **Shutter Speed** | 1/125s - 1/500s | Motion blur on face; frozen unnaturally mid-blink |
| **Exposure** | Balanced histogram, gentle highlight rolloff | Blown white skies, crushed black suits with zero fabric detail |

## Authentic Body Language & Posing Rules

- **Hands**: Always doing something plausible: holding a pen, clasping a notebook, resting naturally on a table, or relaxed at the side. Never clenched into unnatural claw poses.
- **Expression**: Task-engaged, engaged conversational smile, or thoughtful listening. Avoid frozen beauty-pageant grins.
- **Shoulders & Posture**: Relaxed micro-asymmetry (one shoulder slightly higher or angled toward the action). Stiff wooden posture immediately reveals synthetic generation.
